PDF Pro+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how PDF Pro+ handles your information. It is written to match what the app really does. If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated first.

Your PDFs stay on your device

PDF Pro+ works on your device. The PDFs you open, edit, merge, split, compress or print are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see your files. Your PDFs and what is inside them are never shared with advertisers.

What the app saves on your device

The app saves the following on your device only: copies of the PDFs you import and the files it creates for you; their names, folders, favourites and the date you last opened them; up to 5 earlier versions of a file so that Undo works; the page where you stopped reading and your page bookmarks; signatures you save in the app; your settings, such as warm reading mode; and a counter of how many free saves you have used. None of this is sent to us.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. PDF Pro+ does not need an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. We do not run our own analytics or tracking tools. The advertising service described below is separate and may collect information, as explained in the next section.

Advertising

The Android and iPhone versions of PDF Pro+ show small banner ads on the Home and All Tools screens. Ads are never shown while you read or edit a PDF. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as your device advertising identifier, your IP address, basic device and app details, and how you interact with ads. The version for Windows does not show ads. Please read the Google privacy policy to learn how Google uses this information.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before it shows personalised ads. On iPhone, the app may ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites, and you can say no. You can change your choice later from the privacy option in the app. You can also limit ad personalisation in your phone settings.

When a file leaves your device

A file leaves your device only when you choose to send it. If you use Share, Save a Copy or Print, the file goes to the app, folder or printer that you pick. What that app or service does with the file is covered by its own privacy policy, not this one.

Payments

Premium cannot be bought in the app yet. When purchases are added, they will be handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. This policy will be updated before that happens, to explain what is checked and stored.

Removing your data

Everything the app stores is on your device, so you can remove it by deleting files inside the app or by uninstalling the app. We cannot recover your files for you, because we do not have them. For information held by advertising partners, see their privacy policies.

Children

PDF Pro+ is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change too.
